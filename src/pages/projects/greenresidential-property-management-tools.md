Automated the manual processes behind the internal systems at
[Greenresidential](https://www.greenresidential.com), a residential property management company.

### The problem

Property management runs on repetition: tenancies, maintenance requests, inspections, renewals,
payments, each individually simple and collectively unmanageable by hand once a portfolio grows.

The limit that matters is not software features, it is that every manual step consumes someone's
attention. A team can only hold so many properties before the process itself becomes the
constraint, and the usual response — hiring in proportion to the portfolio — is the expensive
one.

### Constraints

These processes were already running, on real tenancies, with real deadlines attached. Automation
had to slot into how the business already worked rather than requiring it to reorganise, and it
had to be right — a missed renewal or a dropped maintenance request has a tenant on the other
end of it.

### What I built

Backend automation in **Python** against the internal systems, targeting the repetitive
operational paths rather than anything customer-facing: the steps that happened the same way
every time and only needed a person because nothing else was doing them.

### Outcome

A team able to manage a larger portfolio without growing in proportion to it, and operational
routine that no longer depended on someone remembering to do it.
