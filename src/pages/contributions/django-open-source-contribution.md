I am an official sponsor of the Django project, supporting its continued development.

### Why this one

The support is not abstract. Django runs in production behind work I have delivered: the
[Artis booking and ticketing system](/projects/artis-booking-ticketing-system/), and the
[SDG Invoervoorziening](/projects/sdg-cms-api-development/) portal through which Dutch
government bodies publish service information for the EU Single Digital Gateway.

Both are cases where the boring choice was the right one. Public infrastructure and
high-traffic ticketing have the same underlying requirement: they outlive the team that builds
them. That makes a framework's governance, documentation and maintainer pool matter more than
its feature list, because those are what determine whether the thing is still maintainable in
ten years by people who were not there at the start.

Sponsorship is the straightforward way to keep that true for the next project that depends on
it — mine or anyone else's.
